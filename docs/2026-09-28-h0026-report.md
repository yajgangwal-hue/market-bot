## The answer

**The bot's own history says: do not add a take-profit.**

For each year from 2018, the test learned a profit target from earlier trades
only.

- **It chose "no target" in 7 of 9 years.** It chose the widest level on the
  grid, 10 ATR, in 2020 and 2026.
- **No trade ever reached a 10-ATR level.** Even the optimistic touch-fill
  version had zero take-profit exits.
- **So the test reproduced the frozen bot exactly:** +58.5889% over 698 trades,
  the same drawdown, the same exits.

**Why.** After entry, this strategy's trades do not snap back to a fair level.
Measured in each stock's own ATR, they move like a random walk with a small
upward drift:

- φ ≈ 1.00 in every year, where below 1 would mean reversion;
- a drift of +0.02 to +0.05 ATR a day.

With that shape, every target lowers expected profit. In every year the
simulated profit rose steadily as the target widened. In 2025, the mean profit
per trade was:

| target | mean profit (ATR) |
|---|---:|
| 1 ATR | 0.20 |
| 2 ATR | 0.31 |
| 5 ATR | 0.46 |
| 10 ATR | 0.49 |
| no target | 0.495 |

It is the arithmetic of the 2026-09-27 research: when the edge builds over
time, cutting the holding short cuts the profit. This method reaches the same
conclusion as EXP-0050 and H-0021, without searching a backtest.

## What it means

- **For the bot: keep the frozen exits.** That is the 2.5-ATR stop, RSI(14) ≥ 60,
  20 sessions and no target. A take-profit re-learned every year from the
  bot's own trades settles on "no target".
- **For "learn with each trade":** the yearly re-calibration *is* that
  learning, done without looking ahead. On about 70 trades a year it learned
  the same thing every year. Re-learning after every trade would add noise,
  not information.
- **CONF-1 is not resolved.** H-0002's 2.0R is 5 ATR. H-0026 never set a 5-ATR
  target, but its simulations rank 5 ATR below "no target" in every year. That
  points the way EXP-0050 does, but it is a simulation, not a backtest.
- **Limits:**
  - The process is a pooled AR(1) on daily closes of the baseline's own
    trades. The simulation carries the stop and the 20-day limit, but not the
    RSI exit.
  - The data is survivorship-affected (H-0023).
  - Decade only.
