## A. P3 definition and decision timestamp

Sealed, unchanged from H-0019:

```
price >= 20  AND  20-day dollar volume >= 50m  AND  stop > 0
AND  RSI(14) <= 35  AND  close > SMA(200)  AND  ATR(14)/close > 0.035
```

Implemented **by enumeration, not by moving the ceiling**: production's own
`evaluate()` is called twice, once with the frozen config and once with
`max_atr_fraction=None`, and P3 = (ceiling-off BUY) AND (frozen STAND_ASIDE).

**Disjointness proven, not assumed:** 4,118 production BUYs seen, 245 P3 BUYs
emitted, **0 P3 emissions were production BUYs**. The wrapper returns
STAND_ASIDE on every production BUY before the ceiling-off call is made, so
the sets cannot intersect.

Membership is fixed at session *t*'s close; the frozen framework enters at
session *t+1*'s **open**. Membership is therefore fully known before the trade
is initiated. The ATR ceiling is still `0.035`, asserted at the end of the run.

| Calmar | 0.341 | 0.167 | −0.174 |
| drawdown duration | 961 d | 1,680 d | +719 d |
| exposure | 44.28% | 45.69% | +1.41 pts |
| trades | 698 | 795 | +97 |

**The mechanism is crowding, and the trade count proves it.** 698 + 242 = 940
trades were available; the combined book took **795**. P3's 242 trades
displaced roughly **145 production trades**, and the displaced trades were
worth more than the ones that replaced them. Exposure barely moved (+1.41 pts)
while drawdown rose 6.94 points — capital was reallocated, not added.

This is the third independent demonstration of the same capacity result
(H-0015, H-0016, now H-0020): **the portfolio is slot- and cash-constrained,
so a new candidate class does not add, it substitutes.**

**Correlation with the frozen book is +0.503** on daily equity returns,
despite **0%** symbol-session overlap at the signal level. Signal-level
disjointness is not portfolio-level independence: both books are long the same
kind of equity risk and compete for the same capital.

## K. Decision

**Does the evidence justify designing a future, separately preregistered
ATR-parameter experiment? No.**

The nomination H-0019 made does not survive the frozen machinery. Three
independent failures: the only survivorship-safe control is underpowered and
negative in two of three thirds; the combined portfolio breaches the drawdown
ceiling; and adding P3 destroys 16.9 points of return by displacing better
trades. The per-observation figure that motivated the nomination was real, and
it did not translate.

**The ATR-ceiling branch is closed.** No parameter experiment is designed. The
12 previously rejected exit/parameter experiments are not reinterpreted and
remain rejected.

H-0019's own caution is now vindicated and should be quoted forward: *a class
being untested is not the same as it being promising.* It was untested, it is
now tested, and it is negative.
