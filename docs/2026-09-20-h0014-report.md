## 1. CLASSIFICATION: **B — INFORMATION EXISTS, EDGE NOT YET DEMONSTRATED**

The intraday representation contains **real, sign-stable incremental
information** over the daily one — it passes the sealed 2-of-3 gate at 3/3 on
all ten snapshots. But the economic case fails at three separate points: the
increment collapses by ~85% once the daily baseline is properly fitted, the
one large apparent opportunity is **not identifiable in real time**, and
portfolio constraints absorb almost all of what remains.

Not **A**, because class C does not survive scrutiny as an opportunity.
Not **C**, because the IC increment is genuinely stable and positive.

## 10. CHRONOLOGICAL STABILITY — the 2-of-3 gate

The literal scheme leaves third 1 unscoreable (nothing precedes it). The same
principle at finer granularity — expanding window by year, each year scored by
a model fit on all earlier years — populates all three thirds. **This was
disclosed before results were seen.**

| snapshot | incr T1 | incr T2 | incr T3 | pooled | **2-of-3** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 09:35 | +0.0387 | +0.0138 | +0.0037 | +0.0166 | **PASS 3/3** |
| 10:00 | +0.0409 | +0.0127 | +0.0061 | +0.0179 | **PASS 3/3** |
| 12:00 | +0.0423 | +0.0067 | +0.0033 | +0.0148 | **PASS 3/3** |
| 15:30 | +0.0441 | +0.0074 | +0.0083 | +0.0173 | **PASS 3/3** |

All ten snapshots pass 3/3. **The sealed stability gate is met.**

**But the magnitude is not what it looks like.** Averaged over the ten
snapshots:

| third | DAILY IC | DAILY+INTRADAY IC | increment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **−0.0107** | +0.0304 | **+0.0411** |
| 2 | +0.0194 | +0.0280 | +0.0085 |
| 3 | **+0.0595** | +0.0657 | **+0.0062** |

**In third 1 the daily baseline has *negative* IC** — the expanding window has
too few training years and the daily model is simply broken there. The large
+0.0411 increment is mostly intraday rescuing an underfit baseline, not
intraday adding to a working one. In third 3, where the daily model is properly
fitted at +0.0595, **intraday adds +0.0062** — roughly 10% relative, and an
order of magnitude below the headline.

**The honest estimate of the incremental information is the recent third:
≈ +0.006 IC.**

## 16. CENTRAL QUESTION 1 — how much information is destroyed?

**Quantified, and it is real but small.**

- **Rank information:** compressing the session into one daily bar costs
  **≈ +0.006 Spearman IC** at the 10-session horizon, measured against a
  properly fitted daily baseline in the most recent third. Against that
  baseline's own IC of +0.0595, intraday carries roughly **10% additional
  rank information**. Positive and sign-stable at 3/3 thirds on all ten
  snapshots.
- **Path ordering:** the order of intraday events is *associated with*
  subsequent outcomes. Best-minus-worst class spread **0.3900%** SPY-relative,
  with stable ordering (`low_high_fade` worst in all three thirds, rank
  correlation +0.49 to +0.77) but **unstable levels** dominated by a
  market-wide shift.
- **Detection timing:** 12,607 sessions show an intraday dislocation the
  closing bar never represents, and 820 daily signals (2.06%) are invisible
  until the close.

**I am not calling any of this profitable.** It is descriptive information
content.

## 17. CENTRAL QUESTION 2 — does it produce a demonstrable out-of-sample trading opportunity?

**No.**

Three independent reasons, any one sufficient:

1. **The magnitude is ~+0.006 IC** against a working daily baseline — an order
   of magnitude below the headline figure, which was inflated by a third-1
   daily model with *negative* IC.
2. **The largest apparent opportunity is not real-time identifiable.** Class C
   (+2.59% SPY-relative, 66.81% win) is defined using session D's close.
   Membership cannot be known at the snapshot, and the return runs through a
   recovery that had already happened. Circular.
3. **Portfolio constraints absorb it.** +225 convertible slots per decade
   (+3.5%), because the 3-entries/day cap already discards 59.4% of existing
   candidates.

Nothing here reaches Stage 3, so no execution assumption was applied and no
cost-adjusted return is claimed. **The phrase "better strategy" is not
supported by this evidence and is not used.**

## 18. SINGLE NEXT RESEARCH DIRECTION

Under **outcome B** the instruction is to identify the missing *decision-rule*
evidence and **not** optimize parameters. That evidence is:

> **A real-time-definable intraday condition.** Every economically interesting
> population found here (class C) is defined partly by session D's close.
> Before any economic experiment is justified, it must be shown that a
> condition observable *entirely at the snapshot* selects a population with
> stable forward SPY-relative separation. That is a definitional/measurement
> question, not a parameter search.

**And a prior constraint that dominates it:** even a perfect intraday signal
converts to **+3.5%** more slots under the current caps. **The entry cap, not
information, is the binding constraint on this architecture.** Any intraday
work should be sequenced *after* that is understood — otherwise it optimises a
stage that cannot express the result.

No experiment is registered. No parameters were changed.
