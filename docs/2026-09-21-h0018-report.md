### The decomposition that decides the classification

| component of the +22.18-point upper bound | value |
|---|---|
| **avoided 0.652% rule-exit haircut** | **+$22,185 — 22.2 pts — 100.0%** |
| genuine price improvement | **$0 — 0.0 pts — 0.0%** |

**The limit level *is* the trigger close.** A filled limit therefore realises
the *same price* the baseline already books. The only difference is that a
resting limit is not charged the 0.652% rule-exit haircut.

So resolving the queue question **cannot reveal better prices**. It can only
decide whether a modelling charge should have been applied to 190 trades.

**And H-0013 already measured that charge on this exact population**: reverted
drift **+0.6113%** against **0.652%** charged, with the charged constant
sitting *inside* the 95% CI **[+0.3748%, +0.8556%]**. MBO would settle whether
to apply a charge that is already known to be approximately correctly sized.

### What cannot be identified

**Portfolio-level impact: NOT IDENTIFIABLE WITHOUT FROZEN-EXPERIMENT RERUN.**

H-0011 established that altering an exit changes later state — **96.4% of
quantities differed, 117 trades differed in existence, and knock-on on
unrelated trades was −$19,062 against +$47,073 direct.** Admitting or denying a
fill consumes cash, occupies a bucket and takes a slot, so no unique
portfolio attribution exists. The trade-level sum above is **not** portfolio
alpha and is not presented as such.

## G. Answers to the required final questions

1. **What is proven.** 28 of 221 could not have filled. Median spread 1.63 bps.
   The full economic span of the remaining uncertainty is **22.18 points, of
   which 100% is the 0.652% haircut and 0% is price improvement.**
2. **What remains uncertain.** Whether any of the 190 would have filled. Queue
   position, depth, hidden liquidity and arrival order are unobservable in L1
   and no volume figure resolves them in principle.
3. **Maximum economically relevant uncertainty.** +22.18 points at the trade
   level, $0 at the lower bound — and **not identifiable at the portfolio
   level at all**.
4. **Can MBO resolve it?** Partially — it would reconstruct displayed queue,
   not hidden liquidity, routing or true arrival priority. Not available from
   the current vendor; requires a commercial feed.
5. **Is resolving it economically justified?** **No.** The question's entire
   span is a charge H-0013 already validated as approximately correctly sized
   for this exact population. Settling it would make the historical ledger more
   precise without creating any new opportunity, and H-0011 — the only strategy
   that would use the answer — remains rejected on grounds this does not touch.
6. **Next highest-value capability.** See below.

## H. Next-step rule — outcome B

Per the registered rule: **close the queue branch as a measurement issue and
identify the next highest-value capability gap.** The queue branch is closed.

**The next capability I would nominate is opportunity generation**, and the
reason is that it is the only major class never tested. Every rejected
experiment in this programme has varied *parameters of one behaviour class* —
RSI≤35 mean reversion above a 200-day average. The candidate generator has
never been asked whether a *different class of market behaviour* is worth
looking at. That is a capability question, not a parameter.

**Two cautions I want on the record before that is pursued:**

- §11's warning is well placed and the evidence supports it. Exposure at 44.28%
  looks like the largest arithmetic gap to SPY, but H-0016 measured the closest
  available population and found cash-denied candidates had **higher raw** and
  **worse SPY-relative** returns. On current evidence the unused exposure is
  more likely correct refusal than missed alpha, and any exposure branch must
  be designed to falsify that rather than confirm it.
- Opportunity generation is untested, which is *not* the same as promising.
  It earns priority because it is unexplored, not because there is evidence
  behind it — and that distinction should survive into whatever is registered
  next.

**No experiment is registered here, nothing is promoted, and no data was
acquired.**
