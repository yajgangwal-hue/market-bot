## Quality warnings — recorded, not repaired

Four bars carry extreme values inconsistent with their own open and close and
with both neighbouring sessions. They are almost certainly bad vendor prints.
**The data is left exactly as acquired.**

| symbol | date | observed | why it is anomalous | file | effect on governed results |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SPY** | **2026-02-02** | **low 69.00 (69.005 in the file) versus close 695.41** | a low one-tenth of the close — a decimal-shift print. The same bar's open is 689.58 and high 696.93; the neighbouring lows are 687.12 and 684.03 | `SPY.csv` | **unknown** |
| NVDA | 2024-06-10 | high 195.95 versus close 121.79 | 61% above the close; the same bar's open is 120.37; the neighbouring highs are 121.69 and 122.87 | `NVDA.csv` | **unknown** |
| MRK | 2021-06-11 | low 15.32 versus close 76.27 | 80% below the close; the same bar's open is 76.71; the neighbouring lows are 74.74 and 74.88 | `MRK.csv` | **unknown** |
| VZ | 2026-01-08 | low 10.5999 versus close 40.57 | 74% below the close; the same bar's open is 40.17; the neighbouring lows are 39.99 and 40.29 | `VZ.csv` | **unknown** |

Also recorded: **1,065 zero-volume bars.**

**Why the effect is unknown.** The emulator fires stops on the bar's low, so a
bad low print can trigger a simulated stop, and a bad high can affect
hindsight peak measurements. Establishing whether any governed trade was
affected means re-examining historical trades, which the reproducibility audit
and this preservation task prohibit. Any future research on this dataset must
state how it treats these four bars, and must not silently repair them.

## Methodology dependency — the 400-bar signal window

Every recorded reproduction of the frozen baseline on this dataset ran with an
undocumented monkeypatch that shows the emulator's signal only the last 400
bars. Details, and why future reproduction must preserve it:
[preservation record §7](../2026-09-24-dataset-preservation-and-research-gate.md).
