# Phase 1 gate: the exact engineering barrier before strategy expansion

*2026-09-15. READ-ONLY — no code, config, scheduled task, broker setting or
registry entry was modified to produce this. Everything below is verified
against the repository; unverifiable items are marked **UNVERIFIED**.*

---

## 0. A DISTINCTION THE PLAN NEEDS AND DID NOT HAVE

"Before we are allowed to research, modify, or add any new strategy" bundles
two different gates. They should not be the same gate:

| gate | what it permits | what it requires |
|---|---|---|
| **RESEARCH gate** | forming hypotheses, building harnesses, measuring candidates on contaminated data with the contamination declared | the engineering items below |
| **ACCEPTANCE gate** | putting a new strategy into the money path | the research gate **plus** uncontaminated forward evidence |

Blocking *research* for three months on a 60-session measurement that
cannot resolve profitability (§9) wastes the three months. Blocking
*acceptance* is what actually protects the account. **This document defines
the research gate. The acceptance gate is §E.**

## 1–2. THE CRITICAL PATH, IN TRUE DEPENDENCY ORDER

Not the plan's order. This is derived from what each item consumes.

```
P0  Golden master            ─┐ nothing can be proven changed without it
P1  Contamination gate       ─┤ must exist before the baseline is re-measured
P2  Liquidity cap in sim     ─┤ changes quantities → changes everything after
P3  Exit-fill realism        ─┤ changes exit prices → changes everything after
P4  Portfolio scope decision ─┘ decides WHAT the baseline even describes
        │
P5  Re-measure the baseline (once, under an explicit override)
        │
P6  Benchmark consistency (needs the bot's leg final)
        │
P7  Purge + embargo harness  ─┐ needed only to judge a NEW strategy,
P8  Per-fold refit walk-fwd  ─┘ not to fix the current one
        │
   RESEARCH GATE OPEN
```

**Why this order and not the plan's:**
- **P0 before everything.** The plan started with the liquidity cap. That is
  wrong: change the simulator first and you can no longer prove which
  differences were intended. A golden master is the only thing that makes
  every later diff attributable.
- **P1 before P5, not last.** Re-measuring the baseline *is itself a use of
  the contaminated sets*. If the gate is built afterwards, its very first
  act is to forbid the thing already done. It must exist first, with a
  declared override, so the baseline re-run is recorded as a sanctioned use.
- **P2 before P3.** Both change reported returns. P2 changes *quantities*,
  P3 changes *prices per unit*. Doing P3 first means its measured effect is
  computed on position sizes that P2 will then alter, so the number has to
  be thrown away and recomputed. One ordering avoids redoing work.
- **P4 before P5.** The baseline cannot be re-measured until we have decided
  whether it describes the equity book or the whole account.
- **P6 after P5.** The benchmark compares the bot against SPY; the bot's leg
  must be final first.
- **P7/P8 last and separable.** They are capability for judging *future*
  strategies. Nothing in P0–P6 depends on them.

### P1 — Contamination gate

- **Files:** `src/event_aware_trader/research.py` (`production_report`, or a
  new `guarded_report` wrapper); `docs/experiments.jsonl` untouched by the

- **Current:** `DATASETS` *declares* decade / thirty_year / etf_subset /

- **Rationale for allowing `rejection_test`:** contaminated data can still

  elsewhere. It can never *accept*. The gate must encode that asymmetry, not
  ban the data.
- **Risk to production:** none — `research.py` is not imported by
  `autotrade.py`. **VERIFIED:** `grep "research" src/event_aware_trader/autotrade.py` → no match.

- **Acceptance:** no path to a contaminated-set score without a registry row

- **Files:** `src/event_aware_trader/portfolio.py` only — the call site at

- **Current:** `RiskPolicy.max_volume_participation = 0.02` is passed into

- **Corrected:** after `position_size`, apply the **existing shared**

- **Why it matters:** a declared risk control silently unapplied means every

- **What could accidentally change production:** nothing — the change is in

  5. ADV uses only bars up to the entry bar.
- **Look-ahead check:** append 200 future bars to the series and assert the
  computed ADV and resulting quantity are unchanged.
- **Acceptance:** all five pass; the re-measured baseline's delta is
  recorded in the registry (expected near zero at $100k against $50M-ADV
  names, but **UNVERIFIED until measured** — it must not be assumed).

### P3 — Exit-fill realism (see §6 for the full resolution)

- **Files:** `src/event_aware_trader/portfolio.py`, exactly two lines —
  **498** (`reverted`) and **508** (`time_exit`).
- **Current:** both fill at `bar.close`. Live sells at the 15-minute cycle
  on which the rule fires.
- **Corrected:** apply a configurable haircut to those two exits only.
- **Not changed:** line 431/433 (`stop` — fill price is not a choice, and
  measured upside is exactly 0.00/share), 494/496 (`take_profit` — a limit
  fills at its level or better), and every default-off variant.
- **Risk to production:** none. Simulator only.
- **Tests:** haircut of 0 reproduces the golden master exactly; a haircut
  lowers only `reverted`/`time_exit` prices; stop and take-profit prices are
  bit-identical under any haircut; the haircut cannot make an exit price
  negative or below the day's low.
- **Look-ahead check:** the haircut is a constant applied to a price already
  in the bar; it introduces no new data dependency. Assert the trade list
  (which trades, which days) is **identical** with and without it — only
  prices may move.
- **Acceptance:** all pass; the baseline is re-measured with the haircut on;
  the resulting CAGR is **lower** and that lower number becomes the headline.

### P4 — Portfolio scope decision (**not** modelling)

- **Files:** documentation + `research.py` docstring. **No behaviour change.**
- **Current:** the simulator models an equity-only book. The live account
  runs that book **plus** a 5% BTC sleeve funded from a 5% reserve the
  simulator does not withhold. Every historical figure therefore describes a
  portfolio that does not exist.
- **Corrected — recommended: SCOPE, do not model.** Declare explicitly that
  all backtests describe the **equity book only, at 100% of simulated
  capital**, and that the live account allocates 95% to it and 5% to the
  sleeve. Report the sleeve's contribution separately, as already done.
- **Why not model it:** building the sleeve into `portfolio.py` means
  merging two asset classes with different calendars (24/7 vs session),
  different cost models, and a cash-competition interaction — a large change
  to the money-path simulator, to make the baseline *different* rather than
  *more correct*. The scoping statement is honest, costs nothing, and
  removes the false impression at a fraction of the risk.
- **Risk to production:** none.
- **Tests:** a docstring/report assertion that any account-level figure
  states its scope. **UNVERIFIED** whether this is mechanically testable
  beyond a string check; likely it is a review item, not a test.
- **Acceptance:** no report emits an account-level return without naming
  whether it includes the sleeve.

### P7 — Purge + embargo harness · P8 — Per-fold refit walk-forward

- **Files:** `src/event_aware_trader/research.py` (new functions only).
- **Current:** `walk_forward_years` slices one run of a fixed rule and

- **Corrected:** fold generator that (a) fits only on data before the fold,

- **Risk to production:** none.
- **Tests:** a trade straddling a boundary is purged; the embargo removes

  later fold; a deliberately leaked fixture is caught.
- **Acceptance:** a known-leaky synthetic dataset scores ~0.5 through the
  harness and ~0.9 without it.

## 5. THE THREE FINDINGS, ACCOUNTED FOR

**(a) Leakage auditor: train 0.9863 vs test 0.5517, memorisation alarm.**
No look-ahead — the shuffled split scores *lower* (0.5026), which is the
diagnostic: a model borrowing the future is helped by shuffling, not hurt.
Severe memorisation. It independently confirms EXP-0029 (0.5501 full-fit vs
0.5056 walk-forward) by a second method.
**Action:** no Phase 1 change. `live_model_floor` stays 0.0. The model is
already excluded from the money path, so the alarm describes a component
that cannot affect trading. It becomes a *blocker* only if anyone proposes
enabling it — at which point P7/P8 are the prerequisite. Add the audit as a
scheduled command (non-blocking, ~30 lines).

**(b) Liquidity cap declared but unenforced.** → **P2**, critical path
position 3. The shared `cap_by_participation` already exists and is used by
live, so the fix is reuse, not reimplementation — which also prevents future
drift.

**(c) Simulator models neither the sleeve nor the 5% reserve.** → **P4**.
Recommended resolution is to **scope, not model**. This is the finding with
the widest blast radius if mishandled: modelling it would change every
historical number for reasons unrelated to correctness, and would enlarge
the money-path simulator substantially. Declaring the scope is honest and
carries no risk.

## 6. RESOLVING THE EXIT DISCREPANCY PRECISELY

**Your question — should the Phase 1 fix make the simulator reproduce actual
live trigger execution rather than improving either side? Yes.** Phase 1's
job is to stop overstating, not to capture the 0.65%.

**The obstacle, stated plainly.** The simulator runs on **daily bars**. At
the moment live triggers an exit — some 15-minute cycle during the session —
the daily bar offers no corresponding price. **The simulator therefore
cannot literally reproduce the live fill.** Any claim to "model trigger
execution" on daily data would be fabrication.

**What can be done honestly:** charge the *measured* difference. From 155
real exits on 5-minute bars, the close exceeded the trigger price by:

| assumed trigger time | close advantage |
|---|---|
| 10:00 ET | **0.652%** |
| 12:30 ET | 0.289% |
| 15:00 ET | 0.120% |

**Exact change.** In `portfolio.py`, at lines **498** and **508** only:

```
exit_raw = bar.close * (1.0 - rule_exit_timing_haircut)
```

with `rule_exit_timing_haircut: float = 0.0` as the signature default (so
the golden master is preserved) and the **production candidate setting it to
0.00652** — the worst measured case.

**Why the worst case.** The live distribution of trigger times is
**UNVERIFIED**: only three rule exits have ever been logged live, all before
exit-quality instrumentation existed. Phase 1's risk is overstating, so the
conservative bound is correct until the distribution is measured. Record in
the registry that the haircut is a bound, not an estimate, and that it
should be replaced by the measured distribution once ~30 live rule exits
have accrued.

**It must not be called slippage.** It is not an execution cost; it is the
difference between selling at the trigger and selling at the close. Naming
it `rule_exit_timing_haircut` keeps that honest, and keeps it separate from
`CostModel`, which is already applied on top.

**Expected effect:** the 30-year headline falls. Applying 0.652% to the 968
improvable exits is directionally the inverse of EXP-0048's +0.73 points at
half-capture, so **expect roughly −0.7 to −1.3 CAGR points.** That is the
correct outcome of this item.

## 9. EVALUATION-DATA PROTECTION RULES

**Reserved and untouchable: the live paper account from 2026-09-11 onward
(`research.FORWARD_START`), currently 3 sessions.**

**Permitted during Phase 1**
- reading forward equity to verify the *plumbing* works — that a trade was
  recorded, that a stop rested, that an example was appended
- reporting the forward record with its NOT-A-FINDING flag
- fixing bugs the forward record reveals, provided the fix has no economic
  effect (a missing stop is a bug; a disappointing return is not)

**Forbidden during Phase 1**
- any parameter chosen or changed because of forward performance
- any strategy accepted or rejected on the forward record
- fitting, tuning or selecting anything against forward data
- using forward data in the baseline re-measurement
- consulting cumulative forward excess return to decide what to work on next

**The exact moment it is contaminated:** when a forward observation changes a
decision. Reading is free; *acting* spends it. Enforcement: any change
citing forward data requires a registry row naming the forward window, which
restarts the 60-session count for that parameter's family.

**Mechanical protection:** the contamination gate (P1) must treat `forward`
as a dataset that no research function may score against without
`purpose="forward_gate_evaluation"`, and that purpose must be unavailable
until the session count reaches 60.

# E. "ALLOWED TO BEGIN STRATEGY RESEARCH" GATE

**Research may begin when G1–G16 are all true.** At that point the simulator
and the live bot agree, every reported number describes the system actually
running, contaminated data cannot be scored without a recorded purpose, and
a leak-resistant validation harness exists.

**Research may NOT conclude in acceptance until, additionally:**
1. the candidate is pre-registered before its result is known;
2. it survives P7/P8 with purge and embargo;
3. its deflated Sharpe is computed against the registry's true configuration
   count with recorded trial Sharpes;
4. it shows a positive per-year distribution, not a positive mean;
5. it clears the forward gate — and note plainly that **60 sessions cannot
   establish profitability.** At 6.7% annual volatility the standard error
   over 60 sessions is ≈3.3% of period return, wider than the entire annual
   edge. The forward gate proves *technical correctness*: the loop ran, every
   position was protected, every exit produced a training example, recorded
   equity matched the broker. **Profitability requires years, and no amount
   of engineering shortens that.**

Anyone who reports the 60-session result as evidence the strategy works has
misread the gate, and the gate's own output should say so.
